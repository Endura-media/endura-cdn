What Drives the Cost of Dealership Cleaning

There's no single price for dealership cleaning, because no two stores are the same size, use the same floors or keep the same hours. These move the number most:

Floor size and type. Porcelain tile, VCT and sealed concrete each need different machines, pads and chemicals.

How often. Daily service costs more than weekly, but keeps floors from falling behind and needing a bigger job later.

After-hours work. Strip and wax and deep scrubbing are usually done after close.

Condition today. A floor with months of buildup takes more work the first time than it does to maintain.

When quotes from different companies don't line up, these questions usually show why: Is floor machine work included or extra? Who answers when something's missed? Which days and hours are covered? Is the crew background-checked and insured?
